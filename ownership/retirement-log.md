## Purpose

The permanent record of every SOP retirement: what was retired, when, why, and what replaced it. No SOP leaves active service without an entry here.

## Rules

- Entries are append-only. Never edit or remove a row.
- Every row must have a reason from the categories in `workflows/sop-retirement.md`.
- `Replaced by` is `none` only for `eliminated` and `obsolete` reasons. `superseded` and `merged` must name the replacement.
- Review this log during the annual SOP review. Patterns here (frequent obsolescence, never eliminated) say something about how the operation is changing.
